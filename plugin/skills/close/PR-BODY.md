# The sentences a reviewer reads first

Read by close in section 8. `vela close pr` builds the rest of the body: the title, the reviewers, the decisions as receipts, what changed, the day the lead said yes to sending, the lines the lead picked to quote, the voice review, what was kept on Claude's suggestion (by name), the overrides, the open and overdue questions, the check and the stage. Don't repeat any of it, and don't quote anyone: what people said stays in the session notes.

Write two or three sentences, in the house voice:

- what the session settled, in the reader's terms;
- what to look at first, and why: a decision that shapes the design, a contract another team uses, a page rewritten whole;
- what is still open that matters to them, and who is chasing it.

Name things; an ID only as a tag after a name. No check codes and no lists. For example: "This settles how the dock service takes payment: through the card processor, not a till of its own. Look first at the change to the payment contract, since the fleet console uses it too. Who owns the Spokewell repair feed is still open; Ines is asking by October 23."

Then add a line for anything the body can't see, only when there is one: each paragraph rewritten whole, pointing to `## Replaced prose` in the session record; each "No examples for <type>: drafted without examples" line house-voice gave; each catalog entry drafted under `## Catalog proposals`.
