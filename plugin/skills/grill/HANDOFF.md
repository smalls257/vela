# Handing an app over

Read when a grill's item is an app's handoff.

0. Run `vela role`. In a reader copy, stop and say: "Only the plan's owner can hand an app to its team, because it records exactly what they accepted. Send <the owner's name> a proposal instead." (`.vela/published.json` names them.)
1. Run `vela handoff preview <APP-id> --for-lead`. Say once what is not met, as one list in plain words, and offer to hand over anyway: the `"handoff checklist"` override, filed with the `override` kind on the app, from the lead's captured reason. Do not ask about each line. Say the not-settled list before step 3's read-back.
2. The team lead who takes the app is named, with a handle. They say yes in person, or their answer is captured with channel and date, or the lead decides for them. If nobody is named, ask the lead first (`MODES.md`, "Unknowns"); if they can't name one, run `vela add unknown` for it and end the session.
3. Read back, by name, what the team lead takes, each with whether it is settled (decided or confirmed) or not: the app, its runtime page, its contracts both ways, its epics, and every open unknown blocking any of them. Then the slices it is on (say what a slice is the first time; `vela terms` has it), the tracer first, each with its status and this app's piece of it: "The tracer, planned: the desktop agent reads today's calendar and shows the prep note." In a plan with no slice pages, read back the first thin path instead (`vela conventions "Releases"`); if it names none, offer to pick one now or hand over as it is. Then the links: "The team will look for the code, the wiki and the tracker items here. Anything to add before you hand over?" Record only what the lead gives. Get an explicit yes.
4. On the app page, write `handed_off: "<team lead's handle>, <today>"` and `handed_off_pr: pending`. If an open unknown asks who the team lead is, resolve it with the name. Commit with `vela close stage --checkpoint`. Tell the team lead their tech design and build plan go under `docs/` here, where the dashboard shows them. Don't run `vela handoff`: close writes the record after its own commit.
