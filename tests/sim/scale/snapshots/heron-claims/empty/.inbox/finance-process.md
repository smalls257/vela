# How expense claims work today

Finance Operations, September 2026.

- Claims are paper forms with the receipts stapled on. Finance types them into the claims spreadsheet, about 9,000 a
  month.
- UK claims are paid with salary, on the 25th of the month, through our payroll bureau, Paylink.
- US claims are paid every Friday by bank transfer. German claims are paid once a month by the Frankfurt office.
- Allowances paid without receipts must stay within HMRC's benchmark scale rates: today 25 pounds for a stay away
  overnight.
- Claims more than 90 days old are refused.
- Month-end close finishes on working day 3.
- Finance systems are frozen from 25 March to 20 April each year for the year-end close.
- The German entity keeps accounting records and receipts for ten years. Its tax adviser, Brandt & Kessler, signs
  off any change to how those records are kept.
- Cost centres come from PeopleHub, the HR system, every night.
