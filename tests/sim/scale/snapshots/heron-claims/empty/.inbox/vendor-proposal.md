# Ledgerline for Marlowe & Finch

Corrigan Software, proposal of 20 September 2026.

- Price: 5.50 pounds per active user per month. An active user is anyone who submits or approves a claim in that
  month.
- Implementation: 14 weeks from contract signature. Implementation services are billed separately at 1,100 pounds
  a day.
- Hosting: all customer data is held in our Virginia data centre.
- Receipts are kept for seven years and then deleted automatically.
- Receipt reading: the app reads amounts and dates from English-language receipts. Other languages are on our
  roadmap.
- Approvals happen in the app. Export to your general ledger is a nightly file.
- Sign-in through Microsoft Entra ID. Mobile apps for iPhone and Android.
