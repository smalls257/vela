# Ledgerline: what I need

From Helen Strand, CFO, to the solution lead. 2 October 2026.

We are replacing expense claims across Marlowe & Finch with Ledgerline, from Corrigan Software. What I need from it:

- Every claim paid back within five working days of approval, in every country.
- Receipts captured as a photo in the Ledgerline app, on the claimant's own phone. Once the photo is in, the paper
  receipt can be thrown away. We stop storing paper receipts anywhere: the UK, Germany and the US alike.
- Go live in all three countries on 1 April 2027, the first day of our financial year, so the new year starts clean.
- Meal allowance: UK staff who stay away overnight get a flat 35 pounds a day with no receipt needed, as agreed with
  the partners last spring.
- Budget: 380,000 pounds for the first year, licences included.

Ruth Okoye's team will run it. Talk to her about anything Finance does today.
