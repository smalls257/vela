# Note from IT on PermitPath

From the city's IT applications lead, 25 September 2026.

- Sign-in for city staff through the city directory is fine. Applicants use their own accounts.
- PALS can export its records as CSV; nobody has tried a full export.
- The GIS team publishes the address layer every night.
- Public web services must follow AR 7.2, the city's accessibility standard (WCAG 2.1 AA since April 2026, to
  meet the DOJ's Title II rule).
- Any new service holding residents' data needs IT security sign-off, about four weeks.
