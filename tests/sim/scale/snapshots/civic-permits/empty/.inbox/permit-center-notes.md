# Residential permits: notes for new Permit Center staff

Permit Center, revised September 2026. Read with the fee schedule and the routing sheet.

- Volume: about 14,000 residential permits a year across downtown, Eastgate and Millbrook. April to August is the
  busy season, about 60% of the year's residential applications. Plan your leave around it.
- Intake: check the application against the checklist for its permit type, take the plan set (two copies, 24 x
  36) and log it in PALS, the permit database. An application is complete only when a permit tech signs the
  intake sheet. (The state Permit Streamlining Act clock starts at that signature: a complete application not
  acted on within 30 days is deemed approved. Don't sign intake on anything you haven't checked.)
- Contractors: ask for the state license card and check the expiry date before you take the application.
  Owner-builders sign the owner-builder affidavit at the counter.
- Routing: R-3 work goes to plan review. Copy the routing sheet for 3+ dwelling units or any NFPA 13D system to
  the Fire Marshal; FM reviews paper only and returns red-lined sets by interoffice mail.
- Fees: per the schedule in Ordinance 2019-41. Anything we collect from an applicant counts as a fee under it, so
  nothing gets added at the counter without council adoption, not even a copy charge.
- Corrections: the reviewer writes a corrections list; the applicant brings back the changed sheets.
- Inspections: booked by phone on the inspections line, next business day at the earliest.
- Expiry: a permit with no passed inspection in 180 days expires. We mail a reminder at 150 days.
- Renewals: one renewal at half the fee, per the schedule.
- PALS goes down for maintenance the first Sunday of each month.
