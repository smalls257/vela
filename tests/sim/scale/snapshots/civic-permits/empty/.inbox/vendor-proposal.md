# PermitPath for the City of Calderford

Tallis Civic Systems, proposal of 15 September 2026.

- Price: 310,000 dollars a year for up to 120 named city staff users. Applicant accounts are free and unlimited.
- Configuration: 20 weeks from contract signature, permit types configured in waves. Data migration from your
  current system is a separate statement of work, at 180 dollars an hour.
- Applicant accounts: anyone can self-register with an email address. Signatures are click-to-accept.
- Applications: when every required field is filled and the fee is paid, the application is marked complete
  automatically and enters the review queue, with no staff step.
- Uploads: PDF only, up to 50 MB per file, up to 20 files per application.
- Payments: card only, through our processor. A 2.75% card charge is added to the applicant's total at checkout,
  so the city receives the full fee.
- Public search: anyone can search by address and see the full application, including uploaded plans, once a
  permit is issued.
- Accessibility: the applicant portal conforms to WCAG 2.0 Level A.
- Hosting: our cloud, US regions only. Training for city staff is included; four sessions.
