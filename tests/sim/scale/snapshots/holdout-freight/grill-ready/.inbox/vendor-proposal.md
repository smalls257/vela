# DropSign for Annick Freight Lines

Corvane Mobility, proposal of 18 September 2026.

- Price: 14 dollars per device per month. Licensed per company device; contractor devices are not supported.
- Implementation: 16 weeks from contract signature.
- The app is English only. French is on our roadmap for 2028.
- Requires Android 12 or later.
- Hosting: all data, including signatures, names and photos, is held in our Oregon data centre.
- The delivery form opens by itself when the truck enters a 1-mile geofence around the stop.
- Receipt images are available in our customer portal. EDI is not included.
- Delivery records export to your billing system as a nightly file.
