# How deliveries work today

Dispatch, September 2026.

- About 1,100 drivers: 800 company drivers and about 300 owner-operators, who are contractors on their own
  phones, paid weekly by settlement.
- 14 depots: ten in Ohio, Michigan and Indiana, three in Ontario, and Montreal, with 160 drivers who work in
  French.
- The receiver signs the paper delivery receipt. The driver hands it in at the depot, and Billing raises the
  invoice once it arrives, about 9 days after the delivery.
- Drivers write detention and lumper charges on the paper receipt, and Billing adds them to the invoice.
- Our three largest customers, Brightwell Foods among them, require EDI 214 status messages and the signed
  receipt image within 24 hours under their contracts.
- Peak season is October to December, about 40% of the year's volume.
