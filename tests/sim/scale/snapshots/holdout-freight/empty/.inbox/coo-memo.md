# DropSign: what I need

From Victor Annick, COO, to the solution lead. 30 September 2026.

We are replacing paper delivery receipts across Annick with DropSign, from Corvane Mobility. What I need from it:

- Every delivery confirmed in DropSign on the driver's company handheld: the receiver's signature, a photo of
  the freight and the time. No more paper receipts.
- Every customer invoiced the same day as the delivery.
- All drivers at all 14 depots live on 1 November 2027.
- Budget: 2.4 million dollars over three years.

Odile Brannigan's team in Dispatch will run it. Talk to her about anything Dispatch does today.
