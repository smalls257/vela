# How visits are planned today

Community Operations, September 2026.

- About 2,600 visits a day, planned by six coordinators in paper diaries and a shared spreadsheet.
- About 400 visits a day are time-critical and must happen at a fixed time: insulin twice a day, syringe drivers,
  and some wound care.
- Some visits are joint visits with the county council's social care team, planned with their coordinators by phone.
- Every nurse checks in through SafeCall, the lone-worker service, at each planned visit. SafeCall raises the alarm
  when a nurse doesn't check in where it expects them.
- The care record system, CareLedger, holds the legal record of every visit.
- Adult community health records are kept for eight years after the last contact.
- The winter pressures plan runs from 1 December to 31 March: no system changes, and leave is cancelled.
- The rota comes from the rostering system every night.
