# Tidewell: what I need

From Abigail Rowe, Director of Nursing, to the solution lead. 1 October 2026.

- Every district nurse plans their day in Tidewell by 1 March 2027, in all four localities.
- Visits are scheduled automatically overnight, so coordinators stop building the day by hand.
- Nurses see their day on the phone they already carry, whether the trust's or their own.
- Visit notes typed in Tidewell become the record of the visit. The paper diaries are shredded once we are live.
- Patients get a text before the nurse arrives.
- Budget: 210,000 pounds for the first year.

Sam Eldridge's team will run it.
