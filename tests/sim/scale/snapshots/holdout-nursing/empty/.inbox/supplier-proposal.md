# Tidewell for Fenmoor Community Health

Brightpath Health Systems, proposal of 18 September 2026.

- Price: 4 pounds per nurse per month, plus a setup fee and training days charged separately.
- Setup: ten weeks from contract signature.
- Hosting: all customer data is held in our United States cloud region. Texts to patients go through our US
  messaging partner.
- The overnight scheduler plans each nurse's day for the shortest travel time.
- Visit notes are kept in Tidewell and exported weekly to your record system as a PDF attachment.
- Data is kept for two years and then deleted automatically.
- Apps for Android and iPhone.
