# Glossary

One paragraph per term: the term in bold, then what it means in this initiative.

**Heron** is the name the solution lead uses for the desk helper the CIO described. The first release is one app on the person's own laptop that reads their calendar and one folder and shows a note before a client call. What else it reads is still being worked out.

**Security pack** is the set of documents the CISO reviews before the pilot can go ahead. It is due in 30 days from the kickoff, and what it must contain is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-007 CISO security pack requirements|not yet known]].

**Pilot team** is Sam Hadley's Client Delivery team in London, about 60 people, who would be the first to use Heron.

**Intune and Jamf** are the tools IT uses to manage the firm's PCs: Intune for the Windows PCs and Jamf for the Macs. Staff cannot install software themselves, so anything Heron needs on a laptop would have to be pushed through one of them, as [[sources#SRC-03|the tenant note]] says.

**Graph app registration** is how an app is given access to Microsoft 365 data through the Graph. It is raised through the identity team, and [[sources#SRC-06|the CISO]] says a new one needs a security review of about six weeks first. Whether Heron needs one is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-013 Registration or sign-in|still open]].

**Works council** is the employee body in Frankfurt, where the firm has about 600 staff. It takes Heron in December and will not agree before it has read the full list of data Heron reads, as [[sources#SRC-05|the council representative's note]] says.

**Pre-call prep note** is the first release the solution lead is leaning to: about ten minutes before each client call, Heron gives the person one note on who is on the call, what was said last time and what is still open. It is built on the laptop from the calendar and one folder the person points Heron at, as the [[04-solution-strategy|solution strategy]] describes.

**DPO** is the firm's data protection officer. The DPO's rule is that a note may name only people who were in the meeting and may carry no remark about anyone's mood, tone or performance, as [[02-constraints#Constraints|the constraints]] record it. The rest of the DPO's view waits for the impact assessment.

**Capture, Insight and Trust** are the three areas the solution lead names for the work. Capture is everything that reads sources on the laptop, Insight works out and shows what matters, and Trust covers consent, identity and admin. Heron sits in Capture with the Insight work inside it, and Trust is a concern that cuts across the apps.
