# Data

What each app stores, which app is the record, and what moves during the change.

## Strategy

Heron reads three things: the person's calendar entries from Microsoft, the files in one folder the person points it at, and the notes or transcripts a call tool leaves behind. It holds nothing off the laptop. Which data it reads, and on what basis, is not settled, and the works council will not agree before it has read the full list.
