# Solution strategy

The first release is a pre-call prep note. About ten minutes before each client call, Heron gives the person one note on who is on the call, what was said last time and what is still open. It is built on the person's own laptop from their calendar and one folder they point Heron at, so it reads no mailbox and does no general document search.

## The core move

The prep note is the tightest of the three first releases the session weighed, because it reads the least and so gives the CISO and the works council the shortest list of data to review. It rests on two things nobody has confirmed. One is that most of the pilot team's work comes from client calls, [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-009 Basis of the 70 percent figure|which is still a belief]]. The other is that Heron can tell [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-008 Call tools in use|which call tools the team uses]] and what each leaves behind, with [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-017 Client Zoom transcripts and engagement letters|client-hosted Zoom calls]] the open gap.

The morning digest and any "what matters today" view are not in the first release, and nor is a hand-in folder that Heron reads in place of the calendar. Two people will not get what they asked for: the product owner wanted the digest as well as the prep note, and the CIO wanted an adoption dashboard, which the prep note does not give. The solution lead tells both, and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-019 Daily folder habit|whether the pilot team keeps pointing Heron at a folder every day]] is a risk to the whole approach.
