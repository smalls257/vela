# Releases

One release is planned so far, the Client Delivery London pilot on 2 February 2027. It reads calendar entries only: [[CAP-001-read-calendar-and-transcripts#CAP-001.FR1 Read existing Teams transcripts|reading existing Teams transcripts]] is on no release and has no date, because its controls wait on the endpoint lead and legal counsel and the CISO review of 4 December is its gate. The digest rows would be dropped, and the prep notes kept, if legal counsel says a digest is a client record. Everything here is an assumption until a Grill decides it, and the date still waits on [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-071 Lawful basis for each source|the lawful basis for each source]], [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-064 Digest as client record|whether a digest is a client record]] and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-076 Council vote on the pilot|whether the works council votes on the pilot]].
