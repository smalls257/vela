# Heron gets its tenant registration

Consent and identity needs a tenant app registration, which the identity team raises once the CISO has cleared the security pack. The request has to go in by mid-December for the 18 January packaging slot, and whether the review fits is open as [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-058 Security review pack|the security review pack]] and as [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-069 When the registration request goes in|when the request goes in]]: six weeks from mid-December ends after the slot.

## Steps

1. The CISO clears the security pack, due 4 December.
2. [[consent-and-identity-building-blocks|Consent and identity]] sends the registration request to the identity team, in mid-December.
3. The identity team raises the registration in [[consent-and-identity-building-blocks|Consent and identity]].
