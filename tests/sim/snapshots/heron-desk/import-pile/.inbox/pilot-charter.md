# Heron pilot charter

Written by the pilot team lead, 2026-10-08.

The pilot starts on 12 January 2027 and runs for six weeks. Decided at the pilot kickoff and approved by the pilot team lead on 2026-10-08: the morning digest arrives at 07:30 local time.

Success means at least 30 of the 40 pilot staff open the digest on most working days.
