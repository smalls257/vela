# Joint board paper: one front door for outpatients

Owner: Joint Programme Office. Presented to the Joint Integration Committee, 2026-09-10.

Both trusts now share a single patient administration platform, so one front door is largely a
configuration exercise. MyFenwick will be extended to Calder patients. The Keystone contract runs to
March 2028. Capital of 1.2 million pounds is approved for 2026/27. Together the trusts have a combined
outpatient waiting list of 70,000 patients.
