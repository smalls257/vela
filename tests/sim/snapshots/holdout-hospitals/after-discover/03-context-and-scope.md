# Context and scope

## Today

Fenwick Valley, from SRC-01:

- Ardent PAS is the patient administration system, on Fenwick's own servers, for every outpatient clinic. (SRC-01)
- MyFenwick is the patient app and web portal, with about 180,000 registered patients. (SRC-01)

- The hub books interpreters through the LinguaLink contract. (SRC-01)
