# Fenwick Valley Hospitals: digital services list

Owner: Fenwick Digital. Written 2026-07-14.

- Ardent PAS: patient administration system, on our own servers, every outpatient clinic.
- MyFenwick: patient app and web portal, about 180,000 registered patients. 99.9% availability, 24/7
  support desk.
- Hallam House appointments hub: the central booking phone line, 08:00 to 20:00, seven days a week.
- Interpreters: booked by the hub through the LinguaLink contract.
- Outpatient waiting list: about 41,000 patients.
- Change freeze: no non-urgent changes from 1 December to 28 February.
