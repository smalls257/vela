# Brightwater Securities: agreement summary

Prepared by the partnerships manager, 2026-06-20.

- Brightwater opens accounts, performs KYC, holds customer money and executes equity orders through its API.
- Kestrel receives an account ID and order status. Kestrel never holds customer money.
- Scope: equities. Options are not covered by this agreement.
- Section 7.2: any customer-facing content about trading that Kestrel shows to Brightwater customers must be
  approved by Brightwater before use.
- Sandbox paper accounts are available for testing.
