# Constraints

What this initiative cannot change. The board has fixed the dates, and compliance has set a ban on live trading that the product lead reports but has not seen in writing. The product lead adds that nothing regulated goes live before counsel and Brightwater sign off.

| ID | Constraint | Where it comes from | What it forces |
|---|---|---|---|
| CST-1 | Demo in January, launch by March | The board, as [[2026-10-09-trading-coach#^c05\|the product lead reports it]]; [[sources#SRC-05\|the CEO memo]] expects the same dates | Fixes the January demo and the March launch dates |
| CST-2 | Compliance said blanket no on live trading year one | Compliance, as [[2026-10-09-trading-coach#^c27\|the product lead reports it]], with no ruling in hand | Rules out TickerTalk placing live orders in year one; whether orders through a partner broker are covered is open |
| CST-3 | Nothing regulated goes live before counsel and Brightwater sign off | The product lead, [[2026-10-09-first-release#^c04\|in their own words]] | Rules out the options that need a compliance ruling nobody has written until both sign off |
