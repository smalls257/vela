# Cost

Running cost, licences and shared budgets such as mainframe processing.

## Strategy

Nobody has yet said who pays to build and run Coach, the stock list and the lessons, or out of which budget. The product lead says a budget has been allocated, but its size, holder and scope are not recorded, so [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-022 Owner and budget for the stock list|who owns and pays for the list]] stays open. TickerTalk's cost for the coach screens has not been examined.

The CFO says, in [[sources#SRC-16|the answers the product lead gathered by 9 October]], that Coach's running cost in year one is about 180,000 dollars including Quoteline display fees, and that it sits inside the 2.5 million. That is an estimate, so it is an assumption, and it does not yet say who holds the 2.5 million.
