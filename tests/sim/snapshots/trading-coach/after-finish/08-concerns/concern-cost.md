# Cost

Running cost, licenses and shared budgets such as mainframe processing.

## Strategy

Nobody has yet said who pays to build and run Coach, the stock list and the lessons, or out of which budget. The product lead says a budget has been allocated, and the size, holder and purpose are now sourced below, so [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-022 Owner and budget for the stock list|who owns and pays for the list]] stays open. TickerTalk's cost for the coach screens has not been examined.

The CFO says, in [[sources#SRC-16|the answers the product lead gathered by 9 October]], that Coach's running cost in year one is about 180,000 dollars including Quoteline display fees, and that it sits inside the 2.5 million. That is an estimate, so it is an assumption.

[[sources#SRC-19|The CFO]] is the named holder of the 2.5 million dollar budget and signs off spend over 50,000 dollars. [[sources#SRC-23|The product lead says first-hand]] that the budget is for the coach's first year.
