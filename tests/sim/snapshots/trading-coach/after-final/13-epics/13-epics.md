# Epics

Coach has two epics, and both are decided: [[EPC-001-coach-lessons-and-list|the lessons epic]] and [[EPC-002-coach-paper-trading|the paper-trading epic]]. The paper-trading epic was decided while two data questions are still open. TickerTalk has one epic, [[EPC-003-reader-identity-to-coach|the reader identity epic]], which is still a draft and delivers the contract that sends Coach a reader's ID. The coach screens in the app have no epic of their own and ship as part of the mobile rollout.
