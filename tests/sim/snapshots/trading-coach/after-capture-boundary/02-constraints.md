# Constraints

What this initiative cannot change. The board has fixed the January demo, and whether the March launch still holds for release one is open. Compliance has set a ban on live trading. The gathered answers describe the compliance consultant's ruling of 2 October as in writing, and the product lead says they hold it, but the vault does not yet hold the document. The CEO's note adds that trading for customers waits for a later release, and that she decides where she and compliance disagree once counsel has ruled. Outside Counsel's written reply adds two more: TickerTalk does not trade for a customer in the first release, and a trade idea for a particular customer goes out only as Brightwater's recommendation. The partners add their own limits: Brightwater's ten-business-day content review, Quoteline's license terms and an agreement that covers equities and not options. The approved opt-in wording rules out training any model on reader data.
