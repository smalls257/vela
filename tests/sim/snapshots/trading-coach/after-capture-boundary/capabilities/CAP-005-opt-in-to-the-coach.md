# Opt in to the coach

A signed-in reader can choose to use the coach, can withdraw that choice, and sees the coach only while the first release is switched on. It is provided by [[tickertalk-building-blocks|TickerTalk]], which holds the consent, and it is what lets [[coach-building-blocks|Coach]] hold anything about the reader.
