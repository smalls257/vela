# A reader passes the ready track

A reader works through the lessons and three paper trades and is shown as ready. [[coach-building-blocks|Coach]] decides whether the reader has passed, and [[tickertalk-building-blocks|TickerTalk]] shows the result. "Ready" opens nothing live: the first release places no real order. Who or what grades a paper trade, and so what counts as passed, is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-051 How Coach grades a paper trade|not decided]].

## Steps

1. [[tickertalk-building-blocks|TickerTalk]] → [[coach-building-blocks|Coach]]: tells Coach the reader has finished every lesson for the stocks they trade, over [[coach-building-blocks#CON-001 Lessons and track|Lessons and track]]. This direction is a guess: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-077 How Coach learns a lesson is finished|how Coach learns a lesson is finished]] is open. Uses [[CAP-003-pass-the-ready-track|Pass the ready track]].
2. The reader places paper trades on three different stocks from the list, as in [[FLO-002-reader-practices-a-trade|a reader practices a trade]], and each is graded. How a trade is graded is still open, see [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-051 How Coach grades a paper trade|how Coach grades a paper trade]].
3. [[coach-building-blocks|Coach]] → [[tickertalk-building-blocks|TickerTalk]]: marks the reader ready and sends that in the reader's progress over [[coach-building-blocks#CON-001 Lessons and track|Lessons and track]].
4. TickerTalk shows the reader as ready. Nothing live opens, because the release places no real order.
