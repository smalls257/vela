# Constraints

What this initiative cannot change. The board has fixed the dates, and compliance has set a ban on live trading that the product lead reports but has not seen in writing. The product lead adds that nothing regulated goes live before counsel and Brightwater sign off. Outside Counsel's written reply adds two more: TickerTalk does not trade for a customer in the first release, and a trade idea for a particular customer goes out only as Brightwater's recommendation.

| ID | Constraint | Where it comes from | What it forces |
|---|---|---|---|
| CST-1 | Demo in January, launch by March | The board, as [[2026-10-09-trading-coach#^c05\|the product lead reports it]]; [[sources#SRC-05\|the CEO memo]] expects the same dates | Fixes the January demo and the March launch dates |
| CST-2 | Compliance said blanket no on live trading year one | Compliance, as [[2026-10-09-trading-coach#^c27\|the product lead reports it]], with no ruling in hand | Rules out TickerTalk placing live orders in year one; whether orders through a partner broker are covered is open |
| CST-3 | Nothing regulated goes live before counsel and Brightwater sign off | The product lead, [[2026-10-09-first-release#^c04\|in their own words]] | Rules out the options that need a compliance ruling nobody has written until both sign off |
| CST-4 | TickerTalk must not trade on a customer's behalf in the first release | Outside Counsel, in [[sources#SRC-08\|its written reply]] | Rules out automatic ordering in release one; counsel's ruling covers the first release only, so it does not replace the compliance ruling that [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-003 Compliance ruling in writing\|the compliance question]] still asks for |
| CST-5 | A trade idea for a particular customer is allowed only as Brightwater's recommendation | Outside Counsel, in [[sources#SRC-08\|its written reply]] | Rules out the coach giving a customer-specific trade idea as TickerTalk's own; it must go out under Brightwater's duty to act in the customer's best interest and its supervision |
