# Unknowns and risks

This page holds what the plan does not yet know and what could go wrong. The product lead owns most unknowns and every risk for now, because the compliance holder is not yet named. The partnerships manager owns the questions that only Brightwater or Quoteline can answer. Two questions weigh most. The first, [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-004 No live trading and broker orders|whether compliance's ban covers orders placed through a partner broker]], is answered second-hand: it does, until March 2028, and the ruling itself is not yet in writing. The second is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-012 Options coverage|whether Brightwater's agreement covers options]]. The product lead believes that if the agreement does not cover options and the ban covers partner-broker orders, [[11-unknowns-and-risks#R-09 No demo and no March|there is no January demo and no March launch]]. Two more questions come from picking a short list of US stocks to teach on: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-021 Legal clearance of the stock list|counsel's clearance of the list]] comes first, then [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-023 Coverage of every stock on the list|whether Brightwater and Quoteline cover every stock]]. The product lead owns the list and the lessons, and the CFO holds the 2.5 million dollar budget. Quoteline has since said that showing delayed quotes in lessons is display use, which its license allows. Its reply of 9 October says valuing paper positions on delayed quotes is display use as well, so [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-038 Valuing paper trades on Quoteline quotes|that question]] is closed. [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-069 Filling paper orders on Quoteline's delayed quotes|Whether filling a paper order at a delayed price is display use]] is still open, together with what an addendum would cost and who would pay it. The product lead says the Coach lead told them the Brightwater sandbox prices paper fills, which would leave only the Coach-service question, but that is not confirmed. The replies also raised two new questions: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-025 Options lessons in release one|whether options lessons are in release one]] and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-026 Counsel and lesson content|whether counsel reads the lessons themselves]]. Brightwater has since said it will not act as recommender or supervisor for trade ideas in year one. Brightwater's written reply adds a ten-business-day content review of every lesson and screen shown to its customers. The product lead believes [[11-unknowns-and-risks#R-11 The content review may cost January|the review may cost January]], and the open questions are who runs it, whether it can move, whether it reaches an internal demo and what happens when it rejects a lesson. One question waits on the mobile lead: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-019 Mobile capacity for March|what the mobile lead said about capacity, and by when]], which they are to confirm in writing by 14 October. The reader ID's stability, out-of-hours cover and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-048 Does mobile's plan cover sending the ID only for opted-in readers|the plan to send Coach only the ID]] are answered. The product lead cannot brief the CEO until [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-003 Compliance ruling in writing|the written compliance ruling]] is in hand, and needs to know by 10 October whether [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-017 CEO and a paper-only release|the CEO accepts an equities-only release]]. The full texts of the Brightwater agreement, the Quoteline license and the Fieldnote contract are [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-056 Full texts of the three partner documents|not held]]. The date on Brightwater's written reply is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-055 Date of Brightwater's written reply|in question]], and nobody has said [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-058 March launch gate and who decides a slip|what the March launch gate is or who decides a slip]], or [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-057 Which team owns each capability after the change|which team owns each capability]] once the teams form. Two data questions hold up the paper-trading epic: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-059 Whether reader data may go to Brightwater and to a new party|whether reader data may go to Brightwater and to a new party]], which the product lead sends to partnerships, and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-060 Lawful basis for the data Coach holds|the lawful basis for the data Coach holds]], which goes to the privacy lead. Both are due on 16 October. A third, [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-070 Lawful basis for sending the reader ID to Coach|the lawful basis for TickerTalk sending Coach the reader ID]], holds up the reader identity epic. The product lead asks the privacy lead, and the answer is due the same day. Two more concern the build: [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-067 Who covers Coach on demo week|who covers Coach in the January demo week]] and [[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-068 Who owns escalation after two rejected rounds|who owns the escalation after two rejected rounds]] of a lesson. The question ledger at the foot of the page records how each question ended.
