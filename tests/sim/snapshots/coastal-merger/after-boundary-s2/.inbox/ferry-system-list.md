# Harbourline systems list

Owner: Harbourline IT operations. Written 2026-06-30.

- SeaBook: web booking engine. 99.9% availability target May to September, 24/7 on-call.
- FERRYMASTER: mainframe reservation system, ledger of record. Vendor contract runs to March 2029 with an
  18-month notice period.
- Halyard CRM: used by the Kestrel Bay contact centre.
- Tidepay: card payments for web, contact centre and port kiosks.
- Tidemiles: loyalty, 1 point per pound, 100 points redeem for 1 pound.
- Customer database: about 1.2 million records. Owner: to be confirmed after the reorganisation.
- Partner hotel feed: nightly file of package bookings sent to about 40 partner hotels.
