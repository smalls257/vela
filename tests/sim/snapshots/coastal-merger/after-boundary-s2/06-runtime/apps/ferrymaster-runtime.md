# FERRYMASTER runtime

## On failure

If Northway ID or the Account link service is down, FERRYMASTER cannot sign anyone in or look up linked records. Not recorded: whether staff or kiosks on the FERRYMASTER side can work without a sign-in.

When the two people who understand FERRYMASTER leave, nobody is recorded who can build or fix its sign-in. By Ferry Ops' word on the phone, with no document, they leave around Christmas or early in the new year, and nothing is written down that Ferry Ops knows of ([[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-014 FERRYMASTER people and docs|UNK-014]]). Ferry Ops says the handover is a CIO question ([[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-030 Who owns the FERRYMASTER handover|UNK-030]]). About five months pass before the 1 June 2027 go-live ([[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-048 FERRYMASTER leavers and HR|UNK-048]]). The vendor contract end date is disputed ([[11-unknowns-and-risks#UNK-021 FERRYMASTER contract end date|UNK-021]]), and the vendor is not recorded as covering this. The named risk is [[11-unknowns-and-risks#R-01 FERRYMASTER sign-in after the two leave|R-01]].

- **Options considered:** Login with a link service (picked); Login only, no new app: rejected, it holds the link in two places, leaves the rail-side link with no owner and puts the link step on SeaBook, which Ferry Ops says is frozen; Ferry sign-in adapter: rejected, its adapter has no named owner and the link step still has no app
