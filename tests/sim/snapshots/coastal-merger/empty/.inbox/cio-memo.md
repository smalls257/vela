# From the CIO: one customer, one journey, one account

Written 2026-09-14, to the merger program leads.

One customer, one journey, one account across ferry and rail by next summer. Northway ID becomes the
single identity for every customer of the group. The Tidemiles and Northway Plus schemes will be combined.
FERRYMASTER retires when its contract ends in December 2027, so nothing new should be built on it.
