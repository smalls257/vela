# 2026-10-06 Shape: what one account means

## Captures

- [thought] (claude) Option A, one login: Northway ID becomes the single sign-in for ferry and rail customers, so one email and password opens both. Each side keeps its own bookings, records and balances. It costs matching about 1.2 million ferry records to Northway IDs and a sign-in change on the ferry side. It rules out a combined balance in the first release and a ferry side left untouched. → park ^c01
- [thought] (claude) Option B, one linked customer: a shared customer ID links a person's existing ferry and rail accounts, and nothing is merged. Each system keeps its own login and data, and a thin layer in front shows the link. It costs a new layer someone must run and the matching of people across records. It rules out a single sign-in and a combined balance for now, and it may leave FERRYMASTER alone. → park ^c02
- [thought] (claude) Option C, one wallet: one balance and one set of saved payment details across ferry and rail, built on Tidemiles and Northway Plus combined. The customer sees one pot of value whichever side they book. It costs settling who owes the balances, the conversion rate and the Tidepay move. It rules out an early date and any build before the money questions are answered. → park ^c03
