The fare-fiddler service works out what you pay.
It reads every rule in its rule-drawer each day.
Only booking may ask it; the gateway says so,
so no kiosk or website can sneak round below.
When a fare rule gets twiddled, the fiddler reloads,
and the very next quote knows the newest of codes.
