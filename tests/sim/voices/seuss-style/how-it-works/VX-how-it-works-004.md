A request hops in through the front-facing door.
The gatekeeper checks it, then checks it once more.
It parks a small hold in the hold-o-matic store,
then pings the old ledger by queue, nothing more.
If the ledger says yes, out the booked-event flies.
If the ledger says nothing, the hold waits and tries,
and it never once tells the front ends any lies.
