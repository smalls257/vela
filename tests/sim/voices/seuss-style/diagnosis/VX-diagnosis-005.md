We have two little ledgers that never agree:
one says it's confirmed, one says "wait and see".
The kiosk keeps cached what the website forgot,
so a ticket is booked, or it's booked-ish, or not.
They are not really broken, which makes it much worse:
each one is quite right in its own little verse.
