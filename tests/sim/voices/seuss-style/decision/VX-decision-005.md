Should we cache it or ask it? We argued a week.
The cache was quite speedy, the cache was quite sleek,
but a cache that says "booked" when the ledger says "no"
is a fib with good timing, and fibs make things slow.
So we ask every time, though it costs us a tick,
and we wrote down the cache with the reason we picked.
