We looked at three roads and we picked the plain one:
one booking front door where the holding gets done.
We said no to the bus-first, the shiny, the grand,
since the bus isn't ready and nobody planned.
We said no to more scraping with sticky-tape glue,
for it snaps every season, as scrape-a-ma-jigs do.
So one service alone talks to the ledger by queue.
